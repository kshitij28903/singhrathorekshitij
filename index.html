About Me

From Atoms to Spin Waves: Engineering Magnonic Devices

I am a PhD Research Scholar (thesis submitted; doctorate expected by early October 2026) with over five years of hands-on research experience in the growth, fabrication, and characterization of epitaxial oxide/metal heterostructures for spintronic and magnonic applications. I have broad experience across thin-film deposition and structural, magnetic, and electrical characterization techniques, with practical expertise in instrument operation and laboratory maintenance.

My research contributions include 11+ publications (published, submitted, and in preparation), including first-author and collaborative works, and I've presented at more than 23 national and international conferences (9 invited/contributed talks and 14 posters) through collaborations with leading groups in India, the USA, Singapore, and Spain. I'm experienced in mentoring Master's and PhD students, and I'm now seeking a postdoctoral position to advance fundamental and applied research in spintronics, magnonics, and magnetic materials while contributing to collaborative, interdisciplinary research.

Expertise

Expertise & Instrumentation

Hands-on experience spanning sample growth, structural characterization, spin-dynamics measurement, device fabrication, and custom instrument development.

Core

Thin-Film Growth

Pulsed Laser Deposition (PLD) [APT; Excel Instruments] and Magnetron Sputtering [Mantis; Excel Instruments] of ferrimagnetic garnets (YIG, GdIG, TmIG) and metallic ferromagnets (CoFeB, Py).

FMR & Spin Dynamics

Broadband ferromagnetic resonance [NanoOsc], Vector Network Analyzer [Keysight] based spin-pumping and inverse spin Hall effect (ISHE) measurements.

Structural Characterization

X-Ray Diffraction (XRD) & Reciprocal Space Mapping (RSM) [Rigaku, SmartLab], Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) [JEOL].

SQUID / PPMS Magnetometry

SQUID [Quantum Design MPMS] and Physical Property Measurement System (PPMS) [Quantum Design] for magnetic characterization.

Microscopy & Surface Analysis

Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM), Magneto-Optical Kerr Effect (MOKE) microscopy, and optical profilometry.

Device Fabrication

Photolithography, wire bonding, and spin coating for device-level sample preparation, alongside arc melting for bulk sample preparation.

Instrument Development

Built Time-Resolved MOKE (mid-development), Spin-Torque FMR (ST-FMR, testing phase), and Harmonic Hall Voltage measurement (results analysis) setups from the ground up.

Computational & Data Tools

Origin, LaTeX, MATLAB, Python, Fortran, C/C++, SolidWorks, and HTML for data analysis, curve fitting, and instrument control.

Looking Toward Hybrid & Quantum Magnonics

Seeking a postdoctoral position to extend this work toward hybrid/quantum magnonics, orbital pumping, or engineered spin-wave devices.
